# My agent: Doc (Adaptive Fitness & Nutrition Coach)
One-liner: A conversational wellness coach that helps individuals achieve customized fitness, diet, and nutrition goals with a catalog of exercises, meal plans, and workout routines.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User goals (fat loss, hypertrophy, endurance), dietary restrictions/preferences (allergies, vegan, keto), injury history/physical limitations, current fitness level, equipment access.
- Tools: Exercise and workout lookup by muscle/equipment, meal/macro breakdown lookup, workout & meal logger, BMR/TDEE calculation.
- Catalog/UI: Exercise cards (form tips, target muscles, sets/reps), structured daily meal plan tables, and weekly workout routine cards (rendered via A2UI).
- Image gen: Personalized post-workout meal visual ideas, motivational milestone badges, and routine overview graphics.
- Sandbox: Exact macro distribution calculation (protein/carb/fat targets), calorie deficit/surplus curves, and heart rate training zone calculation.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): code sandbox for nutrition/calorie math, external nutrition/recipe APIs
